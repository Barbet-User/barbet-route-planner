Retailer data pulled live from Barbet's public store locator feed on every search — this tool never needs manual updates, only the map itself does. Driving distances and the plotted route come from OSRM's public routing service. Candidates are shortlisted by straight-line distance first (to keep things fast), then re-ranked by real driving distance — so the very edge of a wide radius may occasionally miss a store whose driving route loops back in range. Widen the radius slightly if a known account seems to be missing. Uploaded accounts without their own latitude/longitude are placed in two stages: a fast neighbourhood-level guess (postal FSA or city) decides whether they're in range at all, then only the ones that actually land within your detour get a follow-up lookup on the full postal code/address for an exact pin — so precision cost is only ever paid for accounts that matter to the current search. Add lat/long columns to your file to skip both steps entirely.
